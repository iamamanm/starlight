## Shipping

- We ship within **1–2 business days** after payment confirmation.
- Standard delivery takes **3–7 business days** depending on your location.
- Flat shipping fee: **$5** per order.
- Free shipping on orders over **$60**.

## Returns

Because these are one-off pre-loved items, we only accept returns when:

- The wrong item was sent, or
- The item arrives with undisclosed major damage.

To request a return, email **hello@thriftthreads.shop** within 48 hours of delivery and include photos.

## Need help?

Questions about fit or fabric? Reach out before ordering and we’ll gladly help you choose the right piece.
