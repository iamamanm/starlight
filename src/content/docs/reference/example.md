## Size guide

| Label | Chest/Bust | Waist | Hips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S | 34–36 in | 26–28 in | 36–38 in |
| M | 37–39 in | 29–31 in | 39–41 in |
| L | 40–42 in | 32–34 in | 42–44 in |

## Condition ratings

- **Like New:** Minimal signs of wear.
- **Excellent:** Light wear, no visible flaws.
- **Good:** Noticeable wear but fully functional and clean.

## Care tips

- Wash cold and air dry when possible.
- Turn printed garments inside out before washing.
- Steam or low-heat iron for delicate fabrics.

These steps help your pieces last longer and stay looking fresh.
